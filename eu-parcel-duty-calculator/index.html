FAQ

What changed on 1 July 2026?

Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382 removed the duty relief for consignments worth €150 or less. Until 1 July 2028, B2C distance-sale parcels of up to €150 pay a flat €3 customs duty per item, whether or not you use IOSS. After that, normal duty rates are due to apply.

What counts as one 'item'?

Goods with the same tariff classification count once. The Commission's examples: five T-shirts = €3; T-shirts plus a watch = €6. In the calculator, give lines the same 'tariff group' (e.g. the same HS code) to count them once.

Is there also a handling fee?

The Commission's customs reform page lists a €2 Union handling fee on imported goods from 1 November 2026. Carriers decide how they pass it on, so the calculator lets you change it.

Do I still pay VAT?

Yes. With IOSS you charge the destination country's VAT at checkout. Without IOSS, customs charges import VAT on delivery, and the taxable amount includes the duty and the transport cost (VAT Directive Art. 86). Most carriers add a clearance fee too.

What about parcels over €150?

The flat €3 does not apply. Normal duty rates from TARIC apply, IOSS cannot be used and import VAT is due on entry. The calculator flags this rather than guessing a duty rate.

What are product identifiers?

From 1 November 2026, import declarations for distance sales must carry product identifiers (codes C127, C128 and C129, or Y081 where no standard identifier exists). Ask your carrier which data it needs from you, such as HS code, a precise description, origin and EAN.
