    Testing for wooden toys
    EN 71-1 covers mechanical and physical properties (small parts, sharp edges), EN 71-2 flammability, and EN 71-3 migration of certain elements — relevant for paints, stains and finishes. Keep the test reports and your safety assessment with the technical documentation.
    The new Toy Safety Regulation
    The EU has adopted a Toy Safety Regulation that will replace Directive 2009/48/EC after a transition period and introduces a digital product passport for toys. Until it applies, the Directive's requirements are the ones to follow.

  FAQ
  
    GPSR for small online sellers
    
  
    What is GPSR?
    The General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988 replaced the old General Product Safety Directive on 13 December 2024. It applies to consumer products sold to people in the EU — including online sales from outside the EU.
    I'm a US/UK/Asian seller. Do I really need an EU responsible person?
    If the manufacturer is not established in the EU, GPSR Art. 16 says the product can only be placed on the EU market if there is an economic operator established in the EU responsible for it (for example an importer, an authorised representative or a fulfilment service provider). Their name, postal and electronic address go on the product, packaging, parcel or accompanying document, and in your online listing.
    What must my online listing show?
    Art. 19: the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or trademark, postal and electronic address; the EU responsible person's details where the manufacturer is outside the EU; information to identify the product (picture, type, other identifier); and warnings or safety information, in a language consumers in that Member State easily understand.
    How do I import the Shopify CSV?
    First create product metafield definitions (Settings → Custom data → Products) with namespace "custom" and the keys in the CSV header (e.g. gpsr_manufacturer_name). Then Products → Import, tick "Overwrite products with matching handles". Replace the handle with your real product handle, and test on one product first after exporting a backup.
    Does using this tool make me compliant?
    No tool can do that on its own. GPSR also requires a risk analysis, technical documentation, accident reporting and cooperation with authorities. This tool helps you prepare the label and listing information and shows what's missing. It is not legal advice.
    Is my data stored?
    No. Everything runs in your browser. Your last entries are kept in your browser's local storage so you don't lose them; use Reset to clear.
